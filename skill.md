# Raahi — Figma-to-React Implementation Skill

## Purpose
Build the Raahi mobile-first community delivery application by translating the supplied Figma prototype into a faithful, responsive React implementation.

Figma prototype (source of truth):
https://www.figma.com/proto/ReKPtoy2GxxgxUHyvYg8Pd/Untitled?node-id=2-10488

## Product overview
Raahi is a community-powered, route-based delivery platform. A person who is already travelling to college, work, a market, or home can accept a suitable request along their existing route and earn a reward.

Two request types must be supported:
1. Buy & Deliver — a customer asks a traveller to purchase an item from a nearby shop and deliver it.
2. Pick Up & Deliver — a customer asks a traveller to collect an already-prepared item or parcel and deliver it.

Initial launch context: A college campus and nearby markets in India.
Brand name: Raahi
Tagline: Earn on the way.
Core message: "Going somewhere? Help someone along the way."
Supporting message: "Pick up an item, deliver it along your route and earn rewards to help cover your travel costs."

Principle: Do not promise guaranteed earnings or guaranteed coverage of all transport costs. Present rewards as a way to help cover travel expenses.
